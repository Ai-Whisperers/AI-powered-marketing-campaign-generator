# Prompt Visual: Yoga en la Cama (Wellness)

## Contexto de la Escena

Transmitir serenidad, flexibilidad y soporte. Es importante mostrar la textura del colchón.

## Detalles Técnicos

- **Sujeto:** MC en postura de yoga relajada.
- **Iluminación:** Luz rasante para resaltar texturas de la tela.
- **Atmósfera:** Zen, limpio, minimalista, colores crema y blancos.

## Prompt para Generación

> **A serene, close-up shot of a young woman doing a gentle yoga stretch (Seated Side Bend or Child's Pose) on the edge of a premium mattress. She is wearing comfortable, soft knit loungewear in pastel tones. The morning sunlight hits the mattress at a low angle, highlighting the rich texture and high-quality stitching of the fabric. Her expression is calm and meditative. The background is a white, airy minimalist bedroom with a plant in the corner. The image conveys firmness, support, and peace. High-key photography.**
