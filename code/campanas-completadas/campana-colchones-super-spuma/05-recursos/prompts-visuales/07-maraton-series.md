# Prompt Visual: Maratón de Series (Social)

## Contexto de la Escena

La cama como centro social. Diversión, amigos, informalidad.

## Detalles Técnicos

- **Sujetos:** MC + 2 amigas.
- **Acción:** Reír, comer pororó (palomitas).
- **Iluminación:** Nocturna pero cálida (lámparas de mesa, luz de la TV fuera de cuadro).
- **Estilo:** Lifestyle, Candid, "Friends" vibe.

## Prompt para Generación

> **A cozy, fun lifestyle shot of three young women (including the main Latina character) sitting together on a large King Size mattress. They are laughing hysterically, surrounded by pillows, popcorn bowls, and a laptop. They are wearing pajamas and comfortable socks. The lighting is warm and intimate, coming from bedside lamps, creating a cozy evening atmosphere. The image captures a genuine moment of friendship and fun, showing the bed as a place for social connection, not just sleep. Candid style photography.**
