# Prompt Visual: Tu Mejor Versión (Key Visual)

## Contexto de la Escena

Este es el "Hero Shot" de la campaña. Representa el momento exacto del despertar perfecto. Queremos transmitir que el éxito y la energía del día comienzan aquí.

## Detalles Técnicos

- **Sujeto:** Nuestra protagonista (MC), mujer latina joven (25-30), cabello castaño largo y ondulado. Piel natural, radiante.
- **Iluminación:** "Golden Hour" matutina. Luz suave y cálida que entra por una ventana lateral, creando un halo angelical (rim light) en su cabello.
- **Cámara:** 85mm f/1.8 (Retrato). Profundidad de campo suave.
- **Estilo:** Publicidad Premium, Cinematográfico, High-Resolution.

## Prompt para Generación (Midjourney/Flux/DALL-E 3)

> **Cinematic close-up portrait of a radiant young Latina woman (25-30 years old) waking up in a luxury bed. She is stretching her arms overhead with a genuine, blissful smile, eyes gently closed or just opening. Her long wavy brown hair is messy in a beautiful, stylish way. She is wearing soft, high-quality white silk or cotton loungewear. The room is flooded with warm, soft morning sunlight coming from a large window on the left, creating volumetric lighting effects and a golden glow on her skin. The mattress and bedding look incredibly plush, white, and inviting. The background is a blurred, modern, high-end bedroom. The atmosphere is pure wellness, optimism, and energy. Photorealistic, 8k, advertising photography, shot on Canon R5.**
