# Prompt Visual: Siesta Pods (Activación)

## Contexto de la Escena

Visualización arquitectónica de nuestra activación en el shopping. Debe verse futurista pero acogedor, un oasis de calma.

## Detalles Técnicos

- **Sujeto:** La estructura (El Pod).
- **Entorno:** Atrio de centro comercial (Shopping del Sol).
- **Iluminación:** Luz natural del shopping mezclada con la luz cálida interna del Pod.
- **Estilo:** Architectural Visualization (ArchViz), Photorealistic 3D Render.

## Prompt para Generación

> **Photorealistic 3D architectural render of a "Siesta Pod" installation inside a busy, upscale shopping mall atrium. The Pod is a minimalist cube structure, approximately 3x3 meters, with sleek glass walls that are partially frosted for privacy. Inside the cube, we can see a warm, inviting glow, a luxurious white bed, and wood accents. The brand logo "Super Spuma" is elegantly illuminated in white LED on the glass facade. Outside the pod, blurred shoppers walk by with bags. The contrast between the busy mall environment and the serene, zen-like interior of the pod is emphasized. Modern design, clean lines, warm interior lighting.**
