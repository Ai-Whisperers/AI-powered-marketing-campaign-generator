# Prompt Visual: Smart Home (Tech)

## Contexto de la Escena

La cama integrada en un hogar inteligente. Tecnología invisible.

## Detalles Técnicos

- **Sujeto:** MC interactuando con tecnología (celular/voz).
- **Iluminación:** Nocturna, acentos LED (Cyan/Magenta o Ámbar), "Glow" del colchón.
- **Estilo:** Cyberpunk suave, Tech, Moderno.

## Prompt para Generación

> **Automotive advertising style shot of a high-tech bedroom at night. The young woman is lying comfortably on the mattress, looking peaceful, holding a smartphone. The room features smart lighting with subtle blue and purple LED accents on the walls. Uniquely, the mattress itself seems to emit a soft, warm, inner golden glow, symbolizing its advanced comfort technology and core support. The atmosphere is futuristic yet extremely cozy and inviting. High contrast, sleek textures.**
