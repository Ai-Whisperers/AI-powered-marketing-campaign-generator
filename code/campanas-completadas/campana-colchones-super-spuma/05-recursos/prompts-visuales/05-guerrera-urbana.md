# Prompt Visual: La Guerrera Urbana

## Contexto de la Escena

El contraste dramático entre el "afuera" hostil y el "adentro" seguro.

## Detalles Técnicos

- **Composición:** Split Screen (Pantalla Dividida) vertical.
- **Panel Izquierdo:** Tonos fríos, azules, grises, lluvia, grano.
- **Panel Derecho:** Tonos cálidos, dorados, suaves, nitidez.
- **Estilo:** Cinematic Drama.

## Prompt para Generación

> **A dramatic split-screen image. LEFT SIDE (The Chaos): A young woman stands in a crowded city street under heavy rain. She holds a black umbrella, looking exhausted and stressed. The color palette is cold: desaturated blues, grays, and harsh city lights. Raindrops blur the foreground. RIGHT SIDE (The Sanctuary): The same woman is deeply asleep in a cozy, warm bed, wrapped in a thick white duvet. She looks peaceful and safe. The color palette is warm: golden hour sunlight, soft creams and whites. The image highlights the mattress as her shield against the world. Cinematic, emotional contrast.**
