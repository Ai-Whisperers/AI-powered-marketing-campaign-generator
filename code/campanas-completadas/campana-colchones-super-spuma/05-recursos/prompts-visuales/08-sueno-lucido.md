# Prompt Visual: El Sueño Lúcido (Onírico)

## Contexto de la Escena

Metafórica y artística. Representa la sensación de "flotar" o gravedad cero.

## Detalles Técnicos

- **Sujeto:** MC durmiendo profundamente.
- **Entorno:** Cielo/Nubes surrealista.
- **Estilo:** Realismo Mágico, Fantasía Suave.

## Prompt para Generación

> **A surreal, artistic image of the young woman sleeping peacefully on a white mattress that is floating in a breathtaking sky filled with fluffy pink and orange clouds at sunset. The edges of the mattress seem to blend softly into the clouds. The woman is lying on her back, looking completely weightless and relaxed. Her hair floats slightly as if in zero gravity. The color palette is pastel, dreamy, and soft. Magical realism style, high detailed fantasy art.**
