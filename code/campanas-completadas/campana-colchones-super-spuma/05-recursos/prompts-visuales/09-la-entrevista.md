# Prompt Visual: La Entrevista (Lifestyle)

## Contexto de la Escena

Estilo "Behind the scenes" o influencer.

## Detalles Técnicos

- **Sujeto:** MC mirando a cámara, caminando.
- **Entorno:** Pasillo de casa moderna, viendo hacia el dormitorio.
- **Estilo:** Documental, espontáneo.

## Prompt para Generación

> **A candid, 'behind-the-scenes' style shot of the young woman walking backwards down a stylish hallway in her home, smiling and looking directly at the camera lens as if answering a question. She is wearing stylish casual street clothes (jeans and a nice top). In the background, through an open door, a neatly made, inviting Super Spuma bed is visible, bathed in natural light. The focus is on her inviting personality, with the bed serving as the 'secret' she is revealing. Shallow depth of field.**
