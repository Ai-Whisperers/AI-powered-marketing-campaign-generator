# Prompt Visual: Ricitos de Oro (La Auditora)

## Contexto de la Escena

Humor y moda. Nuestra protagonista interpreta a una crítica exigente. Necesitamos dos imágenes para contar la historia (o un split screen).

## Detalles Técnicos

- **Sujeto:** MC vestida como "Business Chic" (Blazer amarillo fuerte, Tablet).
- **Escenario A:** Habitación lúgubre/incómoda.
- **Escenario B:** Habitación Super Spuma (Cielo).
- **Estilo:** Editorial de Moda, Expresivo.

## Prompt para Generación

> **A split-screen composition contrasting two scenes. LEFT SIDE: The young woman stands in a dimly lit, slightly messy hotel room. She is wearing a sharp, stylish yellow blazer and holding a digital tablet. She looks down at a sagging, unmade bed with a skeptical, critical expression, marking an 'X' on her tablet. RIGHT SIDE: The same woman is lying face down on a pristine, luxurious Super Spuma mattress in a bright, beautiful room. She is hugging a pillow with a look of pure ecstasy and relief, eyes closed, smiling. The lighting on the right is heavenly and soft. High-end editorial fashion photography style.**
