# Prompt Visual: Expectativa vs. Realidad

## Contexto de la Escena

Comparativa directa. Humor visual.

## Detalles Técnicos

- **Composición:** Diptych (Lado a Lado).
- **Imagen A (Malo):** Iluminación dura, colores desaturados, expresión de dolor.
- **Imagen B (Bueno):** Iluminación perfecta, colores vibrantes, expresión de felicidad.

## Prompt para Generación

> **A diptych (two images side by side). LEFT IMAGE: The woman waking up with messy hair, heavy dark circles under her eyes, holding her back in pain, sitting on a sagging, old, greyish mattress. The lighting is dull and flat. RIGHT IMAGE: The same woman waking up looking fresh, glowing, with perfect hair and a happy smile, stretching on a firm, new, pristine white Super Spuma mattress. The lighting is bright and sunny. The contrast emphasizes the difference in sleep quality. Advertising style.**
