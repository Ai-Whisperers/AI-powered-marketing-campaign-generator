# Prompts de Generación de Imágenes - Campaña Super Spuma

Debido a la alta demanda de procesamiento, aquí están los "Guiones Visuales" listos para generar cuando sea posible.

## Configuración General (Style LoRA)

- **Protagonista (MC):** Mujer joven (25-30), cabello castaño largo ondulado, natural, radiante. (Usar imagen de referencia).
- **Estilo:** Publicidad Cinematográfica, Iluminación "Golden Hour" o "High Key" (según escena), Fotorealista 8k, Lente 85mm.

## Prompts por Concepto

### 1. Key Visual: "Tu Mejor Versión"

> A cinematic portrait of a beautiful young woman (25-30 years old) with long wavy brown hair, waking up in a luxurious, bright bedroom on a comfortable mattress. She is stretching her arms, smiling genuinely, radiating energy and wellness. Soft morning sunlight floods the room. High quality, photorealistic, advertising style. Focus on her expression of pure rest and happiness.

### 2. La Prueba de la Vibración

> A dynamic shot of the same woman jumping joyfully on a high-quality mattress. In the foreground, close to the camera, a glass of red wine sits perfectly still on the mattress surface, undisturbed. The woman is in mid-air, hair flying, smiling. The background is a stylish, modern bedroom. Advertising photography, shallow depth of field focusing on the wine glass but keeping the woman clearly visible and in focus.

### 3. Ricitos de Oro (La Auditora)

> A humorous, high-fashion shot of the woman wearing a stylish yellow blazer, holding a tablet, looking critically at a messy, uncomfortable bed in a dimly lit room. Her expression is skeptical and professional. Contrast this with a second image (split screen style) where she is sleeping peacefully on a pristine white mattress with a blissful expression.

### 4. Siesta Pods (Exterior)

> A modern, sleek glass cube structure located in the middle of a busy shopping mall atrium. The cube has soft internal lighting and frosted glass walls for privacy. Inside the cube, a comfortable bed is visible. People walk by with shopping bags, looking curious. The brand logo "Super Spuma" is elegantly displayed on the glass. Architectural visualization style.

### 5. La Guerrera Urbana (Contraste)

> A split-screen composition. Left side: The woman standing in a crowded, rainy city street, holding an umbrella, looking tired. Cool blue tones. Right side: The woman sleeping in a warm, cozy bed, wrapped in a fluffy duvet, looking completely at peace. Warm golden tones. High contrast between chaos and calm.

### 6. Yoga en la Cama

> A serene shot of the woman doing a gentle yoga stretch (Child's Pose or Seated spinal twist) on the edge of the mattress. Soft morning light. She wears comfortable loungewear. The mattress edge looks firm and supportive. Textural detail on the mattress fabric. Zen atmosphere.

### 7. Maratón de Series (Social)

> A cozy evening scene. The woman and two female friends are laughing together on a large King Size mattress. They are surrounded by popcorn bowls and pillows. The atmosphere is warm, fun, and casual. Soft evening lighting (lamps). It shows the bed as a social space for fun, not just sleeping.

### 8. El Sueño Lúcido (Onírico)

> A surreal, artistic image of the woman sleeping on a mattress that is floating in a beautiful sky filled with fluffy clouds. The mattress blends into the clouds. The woman is sleeping on her back, looking weightless. Soft pastel colors, dreamy atmosphere, magical realism style.

### 9. La Entrevista (Lifestyle)

> A candid, "behind the scenes" style shot. The woman is walking backwards down a hallway in her home, smiling at the camera as if answering a question. She is wearing stylish casual clothes. In the background through an open door, a neatly made Super Spuma bed is visible, inviting and cozy.

### 10. Expectativa vs. Realidad

> A diptych (two images side by side). Left image: The woman waking up with messy hair, dark circles under her eyes, looking grumpy and in pain, sitting on a sagging old mattress. Right image: The woman waking up looking fresh, glowing, and happy on a firm, new mattress.

### 11. Smart Home (Tech)

> A modern, high-tech bedroom at night. The woman is lying comfortably on the mattress, looking peaceful. The room has smart lighting (subtle blue/purple LED accents). She is holding a smartphone or interacting with a voice assistant device on the nightstand. The mattress itself seems to glow mainly from within with a warm, inviting golden light, symbolizing advanced comfort technology.
