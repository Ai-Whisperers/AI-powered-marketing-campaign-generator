**Media Consumption Habits and Content Preferences in Paraguay for Nestle Audience**

**Executive Summary**

The media landscape in Paraguay is undergoing significant changes, driven by increasing digital content consumption, a shift towards online platforms, and rising interest in diverse entertainment options. This report provides an overview of media consumption habits and content preferences in Paraguay, with a focus on the Nestle audience.

**Media Consumption Habits**

According to Statista Market Forecast, the media market in Paraguay is witnessing mild growth, influenced by factors like increasing digital content consumption and a shift towards online platforms [Source: Media - Paraguay | Statista Market Forecast]. The country's diverse indigenous heritage influences content preferences, encouraging the production of local narratives that resonate with various audiences.

**Digital Content Consumption**

Mobile phones are changing how people consume digital content in Paraguay, with a high mobile internet penetration rate and average mobile download speed of 19.76 Mbit/s facilitating seamless streaming experiences [Source: Explore mobile phone usage trends in Paraguay]. Mobile content consumption continues to rise, with businesses adapting their strategies to cater to this growing audience.

**Social Media Usage**

According to DataReportal, 65.8% of Paraguay's total internet user base used at least one social media platform in January 2025 [Source: Digital 2025: Paraguay — DataReportal – Global Digital Insights]. Facebook is the most popular social media platform, with 72.5% of the eligible audience using the platform at the time of writing.

**Content Preferences**

Young Paraguayan consumers are increasingly gravitating towards on-demand streaming services, reflecting a desire for personalized entertainment experiences [Source: Media - Paraguay | Statista Market Forecast]. Short video content platforms like TikTok and Instagram Reels are gaining popularity among young consumers, with brands that master the art of short, engaging video content seeing their visibility increase significantly [Source: E-commerce Trends in Paraguay - Jarnias Cyril].

**Demographics**

Women slightly outpace men in overall internet usage (82.1% vs 81%), with women tending to use the internet for searching for information on health, education or training, and consumer services [Source: Paraguay's Internet Boom: 82% Of Population Now Connected, 90 ...]. Young adults aged 20 to 34 are the most active users, with nearly 90% of them online [Source: Paraguay's Internet Boom: 82% Of Population Now Connected, 90 ...].

**E-commerce Trends**

Paraguayan consumer behavior has significantly evolved in recent years, reflecting growing adoption of online shopping. Smartphone penetration has reached 85% in 2025, with shopping apps and mobile-optimized websites becoming essential for reaching this connected audience [Source: E-commerce Trends in Paraguay - Jarnias Cyril].

**Recommendations**

Based on the findings of this report, we recommend that Nestle adapt its marketing strategies to cater to the growing demand for digital content and online shopping in Paraguay. This can include investing in social media advertising, creating engaging short video content, and developing mobile-optimized websites and shopping apps.

**Limitations**

The results of this report are based on available data and may not be comprehensive. Further research is needed to understand the nuances of media consumption habits and content preferences in Paraguay.
