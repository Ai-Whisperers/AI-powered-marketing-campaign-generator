# Quick Reference: Nescafé Paraguay 2025

## Brand at a Glance
- **Brand:** Nescafé (Nestlé)
- **Country:** Paraguay
- **Campaign:** "Tu Mundo, Tu Café" (Make Your World local)
- **Target:** Gen Z + Young Millennials (18-29)

## Core Insight
> "El café no es solo energía — es ritual de autocuidado. Los jóvenes quieren marcas que los acompañen, no que les hablen desde arriba."

## Key Challenge
Reposicionar Nescafé de "café de generaciones anteriores" a la marca de café de la nueva generación paraguaya.

## Market Context
- USD 34M market, growing 6% YoY
- 0.3 kg per capita (lowest in region)
- 96% instant + ground, 2% capsules
- 57% consumption in Asunción area
- "Nueva ola del café" since 2019

## Target Psychographics
- Digital natives valuing physical experiences
- Price-conscious but willing to pay for value
- Seek authenticity, reject "traditional ads"
- Sustainability as expectation (not differentiator)
- Coffee = self-care ritual

## Gen Z Coffee Preferences
- 74% prefer cold drinks
- 73% prefer flavored coffee
- 47% choose plant-based milk
- 60% discover coffee through TikTok

## Campaign Pillars
1. **Mi Momento, Mi Ritual** - Chosen pauses
2. **Hacelo Tuyo** - Personalization
3. **Detrás de Tu Taza** - Sustainability story
4. **Paraguay en el Mundo** - Local pride, global reach

## Tone of Voice
- Close, like a friend (never corporate)
- Paraguayan humor (jopará when natural)
- Empowering without being cheesy
- Real, not unattainable aspirational

## KPI Targets
- +25% brand awareness (18-29)
- +20 points "brand for youth" attribute
- 8M social impressions
- 800K interactions
- +12% sales in target
- 5,000+ UGC pieces

## Key Opportunities
1. **RTD Cold Coffee** - First mover advantage
2. **TikTok native content** - 60% Gen Z discovery
3. **Sustainability messaging** - Plan Nescafé 2030
4. **NBS connection** - 700+ young Paraguayans

## Cultural Notes
- Tereré is national drink (not coffee)
- Jopará (Spanish-Guaraní mix) is authentic
- "Humildad paraguaya" rejects pretentiousness
- Value global brands that "understand" Paraguay

## Do's
- Use jopará naturally
- Show real Paraguayan diversity
- Simple participation mechanics
- Celebrate young Paraguayan talent
- Integrate e-commerce

## Don'ts
- No greenwashing
- No traditional ad content on TikTok
- No condescension
- No outdated trends
- Don't forget CTA
