## PROMPT PRINCIPAL (60 segundos - Hero Video)

```
Create a cinematic 60-second commercial for Nescafé coffee in Paraguay called "Che Momento" (My Moment).

The video follows three young Paraguayans (ages 20-28) in their intimate coffee rituals throughout a single day, shot in a documentary-style with warm, natural lighting.

SCENE 1 (0-15 sec): DAWN - BALCONY MOMENT
A young woman (early 20s) with natural curly hair stands alone on a small apartment balcony in Asunción at sunrise. She's wearing an oversized t-shirt, holding a steaming cup of Nescafé with both hands. The city is waking up below. She takes a slow, intentional sip, closes her eyes, and smiles slightly. Camera captures the steam rising against the pink-orange sky. Warm golden hour lighting. She whispers softly: "Che momento."

SCENE 2 (16-35 sec): NIGHT - STUDY BREAK
A university student (male, mid-20s) sits at a cluttered desk at 2am, laptop glowing, books scattered. He pushes back from his desk, walks to a small kitchen, prepares instant Nescafé - we see the ritual: spoon, hot water, the coffee dissolving. He returns to his desk, takes the first sip, and his tension visibly releases. Soft desk lamp lighting. He puts on headphones and gets back to work with renewed focus.

SCENE 3 (36-50 sec): AFTERNOON - CREATOR'S PAUSE
A young content creator (non-binary, late 20s) sits on the floor of their colorful bedroom/studio, ring light off, camera aside. They're taking a break between recording sessions, holding a Nescafé mug with both hands, scrolling their phone casually. This is their private moment, no performance. Natural window light streaming in.

SCENE 4 (51-60 sec): MONTAGE + LOGO
Quick montage of coffee moments: hands wrapping around mugs, steam rising, satisfied exhales, morning light through windows. End on Nescafé red mug with text overlay: "Che Momento, Che Café" and Nescafé logo. Tagline: "Tu momento. A tu manera."

VISUAL STYLE:
- Color grading: Warm tones, slightly desaturated, film-like grain
- Camera: Handheld with subtle movement, intimate close-ups
- Lighting: Natural light sources only (sunrise, desk lamp, window light)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 cinematic
- No filters or heavy processing - raw, authentic aesthetic
- Focus on textures: steam, ceramic mugs, coffee granules dissolving

AUDIO DIRECTION:
- Ambient sounds: city awakening, keyboard typing, birds
- Soft instrumental music with Paraguayan guitar undertones
- No voiceover until final "Che momento, che café"
- Emphasize the sound of coffee being prepared and sipped

MOOD: Intimate, peaceful, authentic, personal. This is anti-advertising advertising - feels like a documentary about real people, not a commercial. The coffee is present but never the focus - the moment is.

DO NOT include: corporate settings, fake smiles, group social scenes (this is about SOLO intimate moments), terere (that's the social drink - coffee is personal), overly polished/filtered look, celebrities.
```

## NOTAS PARA GENERACIÓN

1. **Autenticidad es clave:** Los personajes deben verse como paraguayos reales, no modelos. Rasgos mestizos, ropa casual real (no styling de moda).

2. **El café nunca es el héroe:** El momento es el héroe. El café está presente pero integrado naturalmente.

3. **Sin terere:** El terere es la bebida social paraguaya. Esta campaña posiciona el café como lo OPUESTO - el momento íntimo personal.

4. **Jopará sutil:** El único texto en pantalla debe ser "Che Momento, Che Café" - mezcla natural de español y guaraní que todos los paraguayos entienden.

5. **Anti-publicidad:** Si parece un comercial tradicional, está mal. Debe sentirse como un cortometraje documental o un video personal de alguien.
